# Employee Handbook

## Time Off

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year. Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next year; any additional unused days expire on January 31.

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 10 sick days per year, and no doctor's note is needed for absences shorter than 3 days.

## Parental Leave

All new parents, including adoptive parents, receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Leave may be taken in one block or split into two blocks within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Teams choose their shared in-office days. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from a department head and are reviewed every 6 months.

## Expenses

Submit expense reports within 30 days of the purchase. Receipts are required for any expense above $25. Travel must be booked through the company portal; economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours.
